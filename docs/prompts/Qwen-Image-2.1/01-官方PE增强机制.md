# 01 · 官方 PE 增强机制(Qwen-Image-2.1 提示词增强的宪法原文)

> **一句话结论**:Qwen 官方把「提示词增强」做成两个 9B 微调模型(PE-T2I / PE-I2I),各自的系统提示词就是增强的完整规则书——T2I 一部 192 行的「观察者描述宪法」(八步 + 九条通则,产出约 20 句 400–500 词的英文单段长文),I2I 一部 205 行的「编辑指令宪法」(双语言决策 + 属性解耦,产出单段、无比例、斩钉截铁的编辑指令);照这两部宪法写,就等于手动执行了官方增强。

- 证据基准日:2026-09-23。

## 1. PE 是什么:两个检查点,两部宪法,不可互换

官方仓库 `prompt_rewrite/README`(存档 `research/pe_prompt_rewrite_readme.md`)开宗明义:

> "Two prompt-enhancer models that share one codebase:"(L3)
> `t2i` — "**Text-to-image prompt expansion.** Turns a short, rough image request into a long English prompt that reads like a description of the finished picture."(L7)
> `edit` — "**Image-editing instruction rewrite.** Turns a vague edit instruction plus its source image(s) into a precise, actionable prompt a downstream editor can follow without guessing."(L8)

三条硬事实(均出自同文件):

1. **底座不是官方开源 base**:「Both checkpoints are **fine-tuned Qwen3.5-VL 9B, not the official base model**」(L10)。
2. **双检查点不可互换、没有合并提示词**:「They are not interchangeable and there is no merged prompt: the answer contract is part of what each model was trained on.」(L16-18)。把 PE 检查点换成官方 Qwen3.5-VL 9B 原版「will load and generate, but it was never trained against either system prompt, so it does not reliably emit the answer JSON -- expect `parse_ok: false` on most rows.」(L22-24)
3. **negative_prompt 恒空**:「`negative_prompt` -- always `""`; neither model emits one. The field exists because downstream editors expect the slot.」(L128-129)

类比:PE 不是「一个更会写提示词的通用助手」,而是两个各背一部宪法的专科公务员——你给它短句,它按宪法程序产出一份格式固定的公文(JSON)。

## 4. 增强产出解剖一:T2I 长文的「五件套」

宪法不给你自由发挥的长文,而是规定了一个可逐句验收的结构。官方规定了几句、几词,逐件对账:

### ① 锚定开头句 —— 恰好 1 句,约 20 词

> `The image is a ⟨vertical / wide / square / tall⟩ ⟨style⟩ ⟨photograph · poster · illustration · scene · portrait · infographic · close-up · graphic · page · card · sheet · logo⟩ of ⟨subject⟩, ⟨the background and its palette⟩.`(L48)

- 媒介名词永远在场:「The medium noun is the one part that is never omitted.」(L50-51)
- 句式可换:「`This is a …` or a bare `A vertical realistic photograph of …` work equally well.」(L50)
- 风格词在此点名一次:「Name it once here; you may echo it in the closing sentence.」(L53-55)

### ② 走帧主体 —— 全文躯干,按固定路线走

- 分区图(海报/页面/界面):「walk the regions」(L74),路线 = 背景 → 顶带 → 左中右 → 底带(L76-81),每区给分寸:「Give each region one or two sentences.」(L79-80)
- 单主体图(肖像/特写/单物):走主体 = 背景 → 姿态与落位 → 头脸 → 身体与每件服饰 → 手持 → 边缘余量(L83-86)
- 背景句的位置是硬规定:「this comes immediately after the opening sentence, not at the end」(L76-77)
- 位置短语的硬覆盖:「You will need eight to fourteen such positional phrases, about ten typically, and they must reach the corners, the edges and the centre — not cluster in the middle.」(L63-65)
- 句首配额:「Roughly a third of your sentences should open on the positional phrase itself」(L90-92)
- 段落纪律:「Keep it to one paragraph.」只有真分区结构(panels, cards, sections, slides)才一段一区(L94-96)

### ③ 文字渲染句 —— 每串可读文字单独交代

- 公式:「name where it sits, what it looks like, and what it says: `a bold black headline across the top reads "…"`」(L103-104)
- 直双引号 + 原文字系统:「Put the string in straight double quotes, in its own script — Chinese, Russian, Korean, Japanese and Arabic text stays in Chinese, Russian, Korean, Japanese and Arabic.」(L106-108)
- 样式交代:「Give its weight, colour, case and relative size.」(L108)
- 不可读的不编造:「call it blurred, indistinct, or too small to read rather than inventing letters」(L110-112)
- 图表也算文字:「its axes, tick labels, legend entries, series and cell values are text too: write them out.」(L112-113)
- 无字图整步跳过:「a third of images have no legible text at all, and inventing signage for them is a mistake」(L100-101)

### ④ 光照句 —— 必须显式,不许留白

> 「Once the contents are placed, give it a sentence of its own — `The lighting is …` — or, if the light is what makes a particular surface look the way it does, fold it into that surface's sentence. Either way it is stated explicitly, not left implied.」(L122-124)

独立成句或折入被照表面句都合法,但「说出来」是强制的。

### ⑤ 总结句 —— 恰好 1 句,不许追加

> `The overall composition ⟨is / uses / feels⟩ …`(L130)

- 一句盖四件事:「Cover balance and symmetry, the palette, the style, and the mood in that one sentence.」(L133-134)
- 唯一性:「Write exactly one such sentence — do not follow it with a second summary.」(L134-135)

### 总量(官方原文的数字)

> 「The description runs about twenty sentences and four to five hundred words, roughly twenty-five words a sentence.」(L139-140)

即:**约 20 句、400–500 词、句均约 25 词**;开头句恰好 1 句约 20 词,总结句恰好 1 句,光照至少显式 1 句,位置短语 8–14 个、⅓ 句子以位置短语开头;走帧主体与文字渲染句不规定精确句数,只有「每区 1–2 句」与总量约束。

## 5. 增强产出解剖二:I2I 编辑指令的格式七规

出自 `research/pe_i2i_system_prompt.txt` L186-192,逐条对照:

1. **单段无换行**:「The entire rewritten prompt must be a single continuous paragraph with NO line breaks or newline characters (`\n`).」(L186)
2. **引号只给画内文字**:「All text that should appear as visible, readable content in the output image must be enclosed in double quotes (""). Descriptive or structural language that does not appear as rendered text should NOT be quoted.」(L187)
3. **比例禁入正文**:「**Never include any resolution or aspect ratio information in `rewritten_prompt`** … Resolution and aspect ratio are conveyed exclusively through the `wh_ratio` and `ratio_follow` fields.」(L188)
4. **写全**:「Write it out in full — no ellipsis, no truncation.」(L189)
5. **肯定式**:「State requirements affirmatively ("保持背景与输入图完全一致") rather than as prohibitions ("禁止改变背景").」(L190)
6. **斩钉截铁**:「Be precise and decisive: no hedging, no unresolved alternatives, no vague degree words left unresolved.」(L191)
7. **语言净化自查最后做**:「re-scan every double-quoted string … and enforce language decision (B). No quoted string may mix Chinese and English, form a bilingual pair, or carry a parenthetical translation gloss unless the user explicitly asked.」(L192)

另外两条结构规定:开头必须是指令不是描述——「Lead with the operation, not a description of the finished picture」(L51);身份类不变量靠指图不靠描述——「point at that image rather than describing features in words — verbal descriptions make the model regenerate and degrade the likeness」(L43)。

**与 T2I 的方向性反差**(同一官方两宪法,最容易踩的坑):T2I 制度性鼓励 hedge——「An observer describing a picture says "appears to be", "likely", "suggesting", and offers a pair … Do this often」(T2I L148-152);I2I 明令禁止——「no hedging, no unresolved alternatives」(I2I L191)。原因:T2I 是观察者描述允许猜测,I2I 是执行指令必须无歧义。

## 8. 实际例子逐字实录

### 8.0 先说清一个客观缺口

**官方公开语料中不存在完整的「PE 输入 → PE 输出」逐字对照**:

- `research/t2i_example.jsonl` 与 `research/edit_example.jsonl` 只有输入端 7 行,无 `rewritten_prompt` 字段(本节 8.1/8.2 全量实录)。
- 官方 `prompt_rewrite/README` 的输出示例自带省略号(L100,见 8.3)。
- 主 README 的两处输出是占位符:`"<long detailed English prompt>"`(research/repo_main_readme.md:210)与 `"Replace the daytime sky with a warm sunset ..."`(research/repo_main_readme.md:233);HF 两张 PE 模型卡同样只有占位符。
- 要拿真实完整对照只能本机跑 PE(两个 9.47GB 检查点),本轮未跑、不编造输出。

因此本节实录四样东西:①T2I 官方输入 3 条;②edit 官方输入 4 条;③唯一同 id 示意对照对(带官方省略号);④官方 ComfyUI 模板两条默认提示词全文(官方手写的「已增强风格示范」,非 PE 产物,作替代对照)。

### 8.1 T2I 官方输入 3 条(逐字,全量)

出处:`research/t2i_example.jsonl`(3 行即全量):

```json
{"id": "t2i_1", "prompt": "一只在雨中弹吉他的柯基"}
{"id": "t2i_2", "prompt": "cyberpunk noodle stall at night, wide shot"}
```
